<p>
  2026 年 11 月 21 日，立方星俱乐部在北京航空航天大学杭州校区召开工作进展会，迎来了来自各指导教师实验室的新成员。7 名学生成员参加了会议，受邀担任地面站顾问的硕士研究生 Afiq Sulistya 也一同出席。会议议程涵盖俱乐部组织架构、首个任务构想、实验室规划以及地面站的下一步工作。
</p>
<p class="en">
  On 21 November 2026, the CubeSat club held an update meeting at the Hangzhou Campus of Beihang University, welcoming new members from the faculty advisors' laboratories. Seven student members attended, joined by master's student Afiq Sulistya, who was invited as a consultant for the ground station. The agenda covered the club's organization, its first mission concept, the laboratory plan and the next steps for the ground station.
</p>

<p>
  成员们确定采用按分系统划分的组织架构，借鉴专业卫星项目的模式。在项目管理与系统工程负责人的统筹下，技术工作分为七个分系统，涵盖星务管理、航电与电源、姿态与轨道控制、推进、通信、结构与热控以及有效载荷。成员们正在陆续确定各自的岗位，网站的团队（Team）页面也将随之更新。
</p>
<p class="en">
  The members adopted a structure organized by subsystems, modelled on professional satellite programmes. Under the management and systems engineering leads, the technical work is divided into seven subsystems, covering on-board data handling, avionics and power, attitude and orbit control, propulsion, communications, structure and thermal control, and the payload. Members are now taking on their positions, and the Team page will be updated accordingly.
</p>

<p>
  经讨论，大家一致认为现阶段最具可行性的任务是星敏感器，计划将其作为俱乐部首颗 1U 立方星的主有效载荷。除利用星图确定卫星姿态外，自主研发的算法还拟利用同一组图像探测其他卫星和空间碎片。紧凑的 1U 规格使项目能够在成员的学业周期内完成。本站此前报道过的超低地球轨道（VLEO）吸气式电推进（ABEP）方向仍在研究之中。
</p>
<p class="en">
  The group agreed that the most feasible mission at this stage is a Star Tracker, planned as the primary payload of the club's first 1U CubeSat. Beyond determining the satellite's attitude from star images, an in-house algorithm would use the same images to detect other satellites and space debris. The compact 1U format keeps the project within the time frame of the members' studies. Air-Breathing Electric Propulsion (ABEP) in Very Low Earth Orbit (VLEO), featured in earlier news on this site, remains under study.
</p>

<p>
  地面站工作目前分为三个方向：射频（RF）、接收卫星数据的服务器，以及承载天线的机械结构。机械结构是当前的工作重点。由于杭州地处台风多发地区，天线底座和桅杆必须能够承受台风级大风。为确定设计方案，团队正在调研校园内已有天线的安装方式并查阅相关技术文献，Afiq Sulistya 也为此提供指导。
</p>
<p class="en">
  Ground station work is now organized into three workstreams: radio frequency (RF), the server that will receive satellite data, and the mechanical structure that will carry the antennas. The mechanical structure is the current priority. Because Hangzhou lies in a typhoon-prone region, the antenna base and mast must withstand typhoon-force winds. To shape the design, the team is studying the antenna installations on campus and a range of technical papers, with guidance from Afiq Sulistya.
</p>

<p>
  会议还介绍了实验室规划方案。该方案将房间划分为四个工作区：地面站区、机械装配区、电子与焊接区以及洁净装配区。拟定的基本设备清单目前正在定稿，为后续采购做准备。
</p>
<p class="en">
  The laboratory plan was also presented. It divides the room into four work zones: ground station, mechanical assembly, electronics and soldering, and clean assembly. A proposed list of essential equipment is now being finalized for procurement.
</p>

  如果你是对卫星如何从第一行代码到最后一颗螺栓逐步成形感到好奇的同学，欢迎加入我们。
</p>
<p class="en">
  Students curious about how a satellite comes together, from the first line of code to the last bolt, are welcome to join us.
</p>

<p>
  每一颗卫星，都始于围坐桌旁时共同提出的一个问题。一台依靠星空辨明方向的仪器，能否同时守望那些让其轨道日益拥挤的物体？一个小团队，能否把白板上的草图变成在太空中正常工作的硬件？小卫星是我们选择提出这些问题的方式；每一位新成员的加入，都让我们能够追问的问题更加宏大。
</p>
<p class="en">
  Every satellite begins as a question shared around a table. Can an instrument that looks to the stars to find its way also keep watch over the objects that crowd its orbit? Can a small team turn a sketch on a whiteboard into hardware that works in space? Small satellites are how we choose to ask such questions, and with every new member, the questions we can ask grow bigger.
</p>

<p>
  近日，LEO Sat Lab 召开了一次关键的整合会议，标志着地面站第一阶段的圆满结束以及学生立方星俱乐部组织架构的正式确立。参与此次会议的有硕士研究生 Matheus、Fadlan、Marwane、Dieko 和 Alfred，以及陈松老师。
</p>
<p class="en">
  The LEO Sat Lab recently held a pivotal integration meeting, marking the successful conclusion of our Ground Station Phase 1 and the official establishment of the student CubeSat club's organizational framework. The session brought together master's students Matheus, Fadlan, Marwane, Dieko, and Alfred, along with Prof. Song Chen.
</p>

<p>
  会上，Fadlan 汇报了地面站的最新进展。继 9 月 8 日通过自制原型天线成功接收信号数据包后，第一阶段已正式完成。陈松老师宣布立即启动第二阶段，实验室将转型为固定的、具备追踪能力的 VHF/UHF 测控站，并为即将进行的结构和射频安装制定了明确的计划和时间表。
</p>
<p class="en">
  During the meeting, Fadlan presented the latest Ground Station progress. Following the successful reception of signal packets via our in-house assembled prototype on September 8, Phase 1 is officially complete. Prof. Song Chen declared the immediate initiation of Phase 2, which will transition the laboratory to a fixed, tracked VHF/UHF station, setting clear plans and deadlines for the upcoming structural and RF installations.
</p>

<p>
  为了支持这些不断推进的运营工作，Matheus 概述了立方星俱乐部的拟议组织架构以及新实验室的物理布局。该计划结合了他在巴西联邦ABC大学（Federal University of ABC）Sirius UFABC 立方星团队的实践经验，引入了符合工业标准的工作流程，将工作区划分为不同的区域，包括专用的地面站枢纽和符合 ESD 标准的总装、集成与测试（AIT）洁净区。
</p>
<p class="en">
  To support these advancing operations, Matheus outlined the proposed organizational structure for the CubeSat club and the physical layout of the new laboratory. Drawing on his prior practical experience with the Sirius UFABC CubeSat team at the Federal University of ABC in Brazil, the plan introduces an industry-standard workflow, dividing the workspace into distinct zones, including a dedicated Ground Station hub and an ESD-safe Assembly, Integration, and Testing (AIT) area.
</p>

<p>
  在强调实验室学术整合方面，Marwane 介绍了他的硕士论文提案，重点是超低地球轨道（VLEO）中的低温技术与吸气式电推进（ABEP）的结合。虽然这项具体研究扩展了之前在我们获得的“翱翔杯” ABCube 奖项中认可的 ABEP 概念，但它目前作为他硕士学位的独立学术追求，展示了实验室孕育的理论研究的多样性。
</p>
<p class="en">
  Highlighting the lab's academic integration, Marwane introduced his master's thesis proposal focusing on Cryogenics combined with Air-Breathing Electric Propulsion (ABEP) in Very Low Earth Orbit (VLEO). While this specific research expands upon the ABEP concepts previously recognized in our ABCube Aoxiang Cup award, it currently serves as an independent academic pursuit for his master's degree, showcasing the diversity of theoretical research nurtured within the lab.
</p>

<p>
  展望未来，俱乐部将在近期的会议中规划成员招募与扩充事宜。我们非常欢迎对开发卫星测控站以及立方星研发与制造感兴趣、充满动力的学生加入我们。
</p>
<p class="en">
  Looking ahead, the club will hold upcoming meetings to plan the expansion of our membership. We are actively open to welcoming motivated students who are interested in the development of the Satellite Station, as well as the research and construction of a CubeSat.
</p>

<p>
  此次会议是我们在物理基础设施和团队组织转型中的一个重要里程碑，向我们的最终目标迈出了坚实的一步：提出“Small Satellites, Big Questions”（小卫星，大问题），并最终探索出“Big Answers”（大答案）。
</p>
<p class="en">
  This meeting represents a significant milestone in transforming our physical infrastructure and team organization, taking a firm step toward our ultimate goal: posing Small Satellites, Big Questions, and ultimately, discovering Big Answers.
</p>

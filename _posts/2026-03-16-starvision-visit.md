<p>
  2026 年 3 月 16 日，陈松老师带队参访地卫二空间技术（杭州）有限公司（Star.Vision），同行人员包括王好老师和马闻泽博士。公司副总裁廖振华及工作人员热情接待了参访团队。
</p>
<p class="en">
  On March 16, 2026, Prof. Song Chen led a visit to Star.Vision Space Technology (Hangzhou) Co., Ltd., accompanied by Prof. Hao Wang and Ph.D. student Wenze Ma. The team was warmly received by Vice President Zhenhua Liao and staff members.
</p>

<p>
  参访期间，团队参观了公司已交付的卫星整星模型、卫星智控中心系统、工程师车间，以及位于楼顶的卫星测控天线。双方就小卫星技术、地面测控及商业航天发展等话题进行了深入交流。
</p>
<p class="en">
  During the visit, the team toured the delivered satellite model, the satellite intelligent control center, the engineering workshop, and the rooftop satellite telemetry antenna. Both sides had in-depth discussions on small satellite technology, ground telemetry, and commercial space development.
</p>

<p>
  此次参访为实验室与商业航天企业的合作奠定了良好基础，也为学生了解航天产业实际提供了宝贵的机会。
</p>
<p class="en">
  This visit laid a solid foundation for collaboration between the lab and the commercial space industry, and provided a valuable opportunity for students to gain first-hand insight into aerospace industry practice.
</p>

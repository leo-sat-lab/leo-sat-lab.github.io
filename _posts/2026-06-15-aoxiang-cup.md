title: "Third Prize at Aoxiang Cup / 翱翔杯三等奖"
date: 2026-06-15
---

<p>
    2026 年 6 月，LEO Sat Lab 团队在"翱翔杯"全国立方星及应用设计大赛中获得<strong>三等奖</strong>。这是实验室成立以来的首个全国性奖项。
  </p>
  <p class="en">
    In June 2026, the LEO Sat Lab team won the <strong>Third Prize</strong> at the Aoxiang Cup National CubeSat and Application Design Competition. This is the first national-level award since the lab was founded.
  </p>

  <p>
    参赛作品围绕一颗 1U 纳卫星的总体设计与姿轨控方案展开，涵盖了任务分析、结构设计、轨道规划和姿态控制策略。团队成员在比赛中完成了方案汇报与技术答辩，得到了评委的认可。
  </p>
  <p class="en">
    The entry focused on the overall design and attitude/orbit control scheme of a 1U nanosatellite, covering mission analysis, structural design, orbit planning, and attitude control strategy. The team completed the proposal presentation and technical defense during the competition, receiving recognition from the judging panel.
  </p>

  <p>
    该奖项是对实验室初期研究工作的鼓励。我们将继续推进样机研制和地面验证，争取在后续赛事和工程实践中取得更好的成果。
  </p>
  <p class="en">
    This award is an encouragement for the lab's early-stage research. We will continue advancing prototype development and ground verification, working toward better results in future competitions and engineering practice.
  </p>

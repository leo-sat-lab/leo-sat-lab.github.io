<p>
  2026 年 6 月，LEO Sat Lab 团队作品 <strong>ABCube：An 8U CubeSat Demonstrator for Air-Breathing Electric Propulsion in VLEO</strong> 在"翱翔杯"全国立方星及应用设计大赛中获得<strong>三等奖</strong>。
</p>
<p class="en">
  In June 2026, the LEO Sat Lab entry — <strong>ABCube: An 8U CubeSat Demonstrator for Air-Breathing Electric Propulsion in VLEO</strong> — won the <strong>Third Prize</strong> at the Aoxiang Cup National CubeSat and Application Design Competition.
</p>

  <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/img/aoxiang-cup-certificate.jpg" alt="翱翔杯获奖证书" style="max-width: 100%; height: auto; border: 1px solid #e5e9ed;">
  <p style="font-size: 13px; color: #888; margin-top: 8px;">翱翔杯三等奖获奖证书 / Aoxiang Cup Third Prize Certificate</p>
</div>

<p>
  ABCube 是一颗 8U 立方星技术验证平台，搭载吸气式电推进系统，面向超低地球轨道（VLEO）长期运行需求。作为一种可持续、低燃料消耗的架构，ABCube 旨在验证 VLEO 持久驻留的关键技术。
</p>
<p class="en">
  ABCube is an 8U CubeSat technology demonstrator equipped with an air-breathing electric propulsion system, designed for persistent operation in Very Low Earth Orbit (VLEO). As a sustainable, fuel-lean architecture, ABCube aims to validate key technologies for long-duration VLEO missions.
</p>

<p>
  团队成员：英杰、马闻泽、朱泽宇、Marwane、Fadlan。指导教师：陈松、王好。
</p>
<p class="en">
  Team members: Yingjie, Wenze Ma, Zeyu Zhu, Marwane, Fadlan. Advisors: Song Chen, Hao Wang.
</p>

<p>
  该奖项是对团队在 CubeSat 总体设计与 VLEO 推进技术方面工作的肯定。实验室将继续推进 ABCube 的详细设计和地面验证。
</p>
<p class="en">
  This award recognizes the team's work on CubeSat system design and VLEO propulsion technology. The lab will continue advancing the detailed design and ground verification of ABCube.
</p>

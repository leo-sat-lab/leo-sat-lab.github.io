<p>
  2026 年 10 月 5 日至 9 日，第 77 届国际宇航大会（IAC 2026）在土耳其安塔利亚举行。LEO Sat Lab 博士研究生马闻泽参加大会，展示实验室在超低轨（VLEO）星座轨道维持机动检测与验证方面的研究成果，并与来自学术界和航天产业的参会者交流。
</p>
<p class="en">
  Ph.D. student Wenze Ma of LEO Sat Lab attended the 77th International Astronautical Congress (IAC 2026), held in Antalya, Türkiye, from October 5 to 9, 2026. He presented the lab's research on detecting and validating station-keeping manoeuvres in very low Earth orbit (VLEO) constellations and exchanged ideas with participants from academia and the space industry.
</p>

<p>
  马闻泽在 IAF 空间运行专题研讨会的最新研究摘要（Late Breaking Abstracts）单元展示了论文《基于耗散能分析的超低轨星座轨道维持机动检测与验证》（Station-keeping Manoeuvres Detection and Validation of VLEO Constellations Using Dissipative Energy Analyses）。<strong>陈松教授（Prof. Dr. Chen Song）担任该论文的通讯作者。</strong>论文信息见 <a href="https://iafastro.directory/iac/paper/id/117851/summary/">IAF 论文页面</a>（编号：IAC-26,B6,LBA,4,x117851）。
</p>
<p class="en">
  At the Late Breaking Abstracts session of the IAF Space Operations Symposium, Wenze Ma presented the paper “Station-keeping Manoeuvres Detection and Validation of VLEO Constellations Using Dissipative Energy Analyses.” <strong>Prof. Dr. Chen Song is the corresponding author of the paper.</strong> Further details are available on the <a href="https://iafastro.directory/iac/paper/id/117851/summary/">IAF paper page</a> (paper code: IAC-26,B6,LBA,4,x117851).
</p>

<p>
  大会期间，马闻泽参加了土耳其总统雷杰普·塔伊普·埃尔多安出席并致辞的开幕式，并有机会见到中国首位航天员杨利伟博士。这些经历让他在展示研究成果之外，也近距离感受到国际航天交流的氛围。
</p>
<p class="en">
  During the congress, Wenze Ma attended the opening ceremony, at which Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan delivered an address. He also had the opportunity to meet Dr. Yang Liwei, China's first astronaut. These experiences complemented the research presentation with a closer view of the international space community.
</p>

<p>
  马闻泽还参观了地卫二（STAR.VISION）的大会展位，了解公司的最新展示。此次展位参观延续了实验室在 <a href="https://leo-sat-lab.github.io/2026/03/16/starvision-visit/">2026 年 3 月参访地卫二</a>时对商业航天发展的关注。据 <a href="https://star.vision/zh-CN/newsroom/4520261008">地卫二的 IAC 2026 新闻报道</a>，公司在大会期间举行了东方慧眼星座“四星联合发布会”，介绍高算力双星与阿曼双星，展示其在太空计算和对地观测方面的最新进展。
</p>
<p class="en">
  Wenze Ma also visited the STAR.VISION exhibition booth to learn about the company's latest exhibits, building on the lab's interest in commercial space developments following its <a href="https://leo-sat-lab.github.io/2026/03/16/starvision-visit/">March 2026 visit to STAR.VISION</a>. According to <a href="https://star.vision/zh-CN/newsroom/4520261008">the company's IAC 2026 announcement</a>, STAR.VISION held a joint unveiling of four satellites for its Oriental Smart Eye constellation: a high-computing-power pair and an Oman pair, highlighting its latest work in space computing and Earth observation.
</p>

  此次参会为实验室展示超低轨星座研究成果、与国际同行交流提供了机会，也帮助学生将科研问题与航天产业的发展联系起来。
</p>
<p class="en">
  Participation in IAC 2026 gave the lab an opportunity to share its VLEO constellation research with international peers and helped connect the student's research with developments in the space industry.

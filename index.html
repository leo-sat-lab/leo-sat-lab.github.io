Research / 研究方向

Satellite System Design

CubeSat 平台架构、任务分析与轨道设计，从需求出发定义每一颗卫星的边界。

CubeSat platform architecture, mission analysis, and orbit design — defining the boundaries of each satellite from requirements.

Attitude & Orbit Control

姿态确定与控制、磁力矩器与反作用轮，用有限的资源实现稳定的在轨指向。

Attitude determination and control, magnetic torquers and reaction wheels — achieving stable on-orbit pointing with limited resources.

Ground Verification

半物理仿真、气浮台试验与热真空测试，在发射前把故障留给地面。

Hardware-in-the-loop simulation, air-bearing tests, and thermal vacuum testing — leave the failures on the ground before launch.
